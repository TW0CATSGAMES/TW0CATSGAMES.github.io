## Privacy Policy
Privacy Policy v1.0, 2026-09-23

This Privacy Policy is based on the [Privacy Policy](https://chris.boyle.name/projects/android-puzzles/privacy/) of the existing port of Simon Tatham’s Portable Puzzle Collection by Chris Boyle. The application itself is not based on Chris Boyle's port.

- This policy covers an Android version of Simon Tatham’s Portable Puzzle Collection, as ported by Thomas Walker. Several other ports of this application exist on Google Play, which are not controlled by Thomas Walker. If in doubt, check the author on the Google Play listing.
- The application does not contain advertising.
- The state of each game and any preferences set within the application are stored on your device, and Android may automatically back these up to your Google account and restore them onto your future devices if your devices support this.
- If the application crashes on your device, as with most Android applications, Android may collect automated diagnostic information to be sent to Google and viewed by Thomas Walker in the Google Play Console.
- The application itself does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information in current versions.
